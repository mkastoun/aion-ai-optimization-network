# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Critique evidence and code without
personal attacks. Welcome questions and differing levels of experience.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate exposure of private
information and repeated disruptive conduct are unacceptable.

Respect source ownership and confidentiality. Do not publish someone else's
personal information or private experiment data without permission. Represent
test results, limitations and scientific claims honestly.

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer through a contact
they have explicitly provided. A private contact is not configured in this local
checkout; the publishing owner must provide one. Do not post sensitive details
in public issues. Maintainers should assess reports fairly, explain decisions
where appropriate and use proportionate actions, including warnings or removal
of abusive content or participation.

This policy applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions and other
spaces where people represent AION.
