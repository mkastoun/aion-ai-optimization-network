# Roadmap

## AION 0.6 release and portability preparation

Prepare a reviewed public source tree, existing MIT licensing, community/security
documents, isolated wheel/sdist validation and Linux/Windows/macOS ARM64 CI.
Apple Silicon capability handling is implemented; actual M4 testing remains
pending. See [release readiness](docs/release-readiness.md),
[publication procedure](docs/github-publication.md) and
[Mac installation](docs/install-macos-apple-silicon.md). This work retains
`0.6.0.dev0` and does not introduce AION 0.7 or new optimization algorithms.

## AION 0.6 MVP Preview: engineering workflow

The preview adds workload selection, deterministic ranking, optional external AI
proposal import, inspectable approved plans, real bounded C++ execution and local
JSON/Markdown/HTML reports. It supports all ten workloads through approved IDs.
It is an engineering prototype; **Phase 6 scientific readiness remains unchanged**.
No model-generated execution, automatic rewriting or research preset promotion
is allowed. See [MVP overview](docs/mvp-overview.md) and [validation](docs/mvp-validation.md).

## Phase 5 implemented; native scientific milestone pending

The 0.5 implementation adds a fixed 17-panel records/logs/matrix research matrix,
trusted source variants and independent distribution oracles, sealed declarations,
explicit command/time budgets, preserved attempts/archives, separate confirmation,
diagnostics and research reports. Original statistics and ten workload contracts
remain intact. Native replication supports separately documented observation
windows, distribution controls, all-ten comparisons/validation and source-linked
reporting. See [methodology](docs/phase5-methodology.md), [validation](docs/phase5-validation.md),
[findings](docs/phase5-research-findings.md) and [native instructions](docs/phase5-native-instructions.md).

Native scientific validation remains pending until real physical Linux runs pass
their applicable controls and independent quality gates. WSL and CI cannot satisfy
it. A valid research outcome is no independently confirmed optimization improvement.

## Phase 6 readiness remains separate

Require validated native measurement conditions, adequate source-variant
correctness, reproducible independent experiments, bounded auditable search,
explicit computational budgets and separation from approved presets. No AI
optimizer is implemented or authorized by a research report.

## Continuing measurement work

Phase 4 implements native/WSL/VM sessions/preflight, optional counters/hot-region sampling, stratified control quality reports, separate ASan/UBSan/differential validation and ten workloads. See [Phase 4 validation](docs/phase4-validation.md), [performance](docs/phase4-performance.md) and [native replication](docs/native-linux-benchmarking.md). Native performance validation stays pending when evidence is only WSL2. Preserve negative/inconclusive results and existing gates. AI optimization stays deferred until controls and independent evidence justify it.

## Measurement quality

Replicate the implemented controls, counters and sanitizers on documented native sessions. Broaden control counts/distributions, study dependence/interval coverage, add repeated compilation observations, microcode metadata and memory/code-size budgets. Test one-factor explanations for records/LTO, logs/PGO and matrix LTO/PGO+LTO; static changes and sampled regions are not causal proof. Native Windows limits/accounting remain future work.

## Research interface

After a separately reviewed readiness decision, consider an optional offline AI candidate provider behind the existing protocol. Keep flag policy, execution, verification, and publication independent. Store provenance and failed proposals. Research source variants remain outside the flag-only preset path; broader transformations require stronger equivalence coverage and review.

## Broader workloads and adapters

Broaden the ten-workload suite's distributions, sizes, PGO training/held-out domains, property-based testing and floating-point contracts. Replicate compiler behavior across versions/systems before source-transformation search. Introduce Rust and Go adapters before PHP runtime tuning. Do not assume flag sets generalize or these programs cover all production software.

## Public distribution

Design canonical signed manifests, independent builders, publisher trust/revocation, evidence replication, compatibility resolution, and explicit opt-in updates. Keep discovery offline and preset application local. Cloud infrastructure, distributed research, and scheduled AI jobs remain outside Phase 5.
